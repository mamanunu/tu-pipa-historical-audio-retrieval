# Data availability and access restrictions

The historical recordings are authorized for research analysis but are not distributed with this repository. The following materials are withheld because of recording permissions and performer rights:

- Digital transfers of the original tapes.
- Four-second analysis clips and audio used for manual replacement review.
- Detailed tables linking titles, performers, source filenames, and exact time positions.
- Listening notes and internal review records.
- Segment-level acoustic features, fitted scaling parameters, downloadable per-segment PCA scores, and per-query retrieval lists.

The repository provides the method code, parameter descriptions, aggregate statistics, and paper figures without identity mappings. `figure_pca_abc.png` shows the paper's scatter plot without title, performer, or segment identifiers and is not accompanied by its per-segment coordinates. The code retains checks for the paper's 54-recording corpus and its 45/9 category structure. Researchers may use it to review similarly structured material for which they hold appropriate authorization, or adapt it for other corpora. Users are responsible for confirming the rights, performer interests, consent, and data-protection requirements applicable to any input material.
